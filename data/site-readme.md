# Getting Started

Welcome to your Open Course Space. It comes with three example courses – make one your own, then remove the ones you don't need.

## Make a Course Your Own

1. **Open** Pages, then a course – CPT363-1 is the simplest to start from
2. **Rename** it with **Course Title**, and update **Course Card Description** and **Instructor(s)** on its **Course** tab
3. **Pick** a **Course Color** for its navbar, and replace the image in its **Header Image** page
4. **Update** this week's reminders: open the course's **Home** page, then its **Reminders** and **Preparations** parts
5. **Edit** the course's other pages – Modules, Schedule, Syllabus – in the same way

## Remove What You Don't Need

- **An example course:** delete its page, or turn off **Show on Courses Page** to keep it out of sight as a reference
- **A page in a course's menu:** turn on **Hide from Menu** on its **Advanced** tab, or delete it
- **The site's Blog or About the Courses page:** delete it, or turn on **Hide from Menu**
