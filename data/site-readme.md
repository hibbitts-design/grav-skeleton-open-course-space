# Getting Started

Welcome to your Open Course Space. It comes with three example courses – adapt one, then remove the ones you don't need.

**How editing works** – open a page in **Pages**, change its text on the **Content** tab and its settings on the other tabs, then **Save**. Text is written in Markdown, such as `**bold**`, `## Heading` and `- list item`.

## Start with Your Site

1. **Name** your site – set **Site Title** in **Configuration → Site**
2. **Add** your logo and colour – set **Custom Logo** and **Accent Color** in **Themes → My Theme**
3. **Change** the welcome text on the **Courses** page

## Make a Course Your Own

1. **Open** a course in **Pages** – the **Sample Course** has writing prompts on every page and is switched off, so turn on **Published** to see it; or adapt CPT363-1, a full example
2. **Rename** it with **Course Title**, and update its **Course Card Description** and **Instructor(s)** on its **Course** tab
3. **Pick** a **Course Color** for its navbar, on the same tab
4. **Swap** the header picture – in its **Header Image** page, add yours under **Page Media** and delete the old one
5. **Update** this week's reminders – open its **Home** page, then the **Reminders** and **Preparations** parts
6. **Edit** its other pages, such as Modules, Schedule and Syllabus, in the same way – **Add Page** creates new ones

For another course, use **Copy page** on a course you've set up.

## Remove What You Don't Need

- **An example course** – delete it, or turn off **Show on Courses Page** to keep it as a reference
- **A page in a course's menu** – delete it, or turn on **Hide from Menu** on its **Advanced** tab
- **The site's Blog or About the Courses page** – delete it, or turn on **Hide from Menu**

For every setting and more how-tos, see the [Open Course Space README](https://github.com/hibbitts-design/grav-skeleton-open-course-space#readme) on GitHub.
