Next week we plan and run a short usability test – here are a few things to do beforehand.

===

* Read through [Module 5](/cpt363-intermediate/modules/module-05) on planning and running usability tests
* Pick two or three tasks that matter most to the people who will use your design
* Ask a friend to try your tasks first, so you can fix anything unclear before the real test
