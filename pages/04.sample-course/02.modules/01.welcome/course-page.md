Welcome students to the course in a sentence or two – who you are, and what they can look forward to.

## How This Course Works

- **Modules** – the course is organised into modules, one a week, each with readings and activities
- **This week's reminders** – the course home page shows what's happening this week and next
- **Assignments** – submitted in your LMS, linked from the sidebar

## Before You Start

1. **Read** the [Syllabus](../../syllabus)
2. **Look over** the [Schedule](../../schedule) for the weeks ahead
3. **Begin** with Module 1
